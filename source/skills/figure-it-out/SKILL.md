# Figure it out

## Frame

State the requested outcome, current evidence, constraints, and the smallest unknown that blocks a choice. Identify which sources are live and which are historical.

## Design the loop

Break work into verifiable units with a concrete observation after each. Put irreversible or external writes behind the applicable authorization gate. Choose a short first experiment that distinguishes the leading hypotheses. Keep independent work moving while another check runs.

## Run and record

Inspect the result, update the hypothesis, and choose the next step. Stop repeating an experiment that returns the same failure. Keep a compact record of command, input, result, and decision. Use [Attack the Premise](../principle-attack-the-premise/SKILL.md) when repeated fixes fail under the same assumption.

## Hand back

Verify the final behavior at the relevant boundary. State the outcome, exact evidence, and unresolved dependency or owner. Do not turn uncertainty into a delivery claim.
