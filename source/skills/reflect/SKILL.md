# Reflect

## Select evidence

Use only the current task's authorized transcript or a user-provided digest. Never scan unrelated chat histories. Identify repeated tool failures, wrong assumptions, missed evidence, and avoidable review burden.

## Review and synthesize

Give the same bounded record to read-only reviewers in `reflect tooling` and `reflect judgment, divergent, synthesizer` roles from [host routing](../repo-pstack-mode/references/host-routing.md). Ask for cited observations and structural fixes, not generic advice. Compare proposals against the actual task evidence. Classify accepted, rejected, and backlog items with reasons.

## Apply

Show a concrete proposed change before altering shared instructions or tooling. Apply only authorized edits to owned paths, then test the resulting workflow or configuration. Report what changed and what remains hypothetical. A conversation lesson does not by itself authorize broad configuration or external writes.
