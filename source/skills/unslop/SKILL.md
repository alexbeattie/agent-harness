# Unslop

Read the intended audience and the concrete result. Put the verdict in the first sentence. Name symbols, inputs, dates, and observed checks when they matter. Remove throat-clearing, repeated summaries, unsupported benefits, invented certainty, uniform bullet structures, and generic risk lists. Keep a real limitation where it changes the reader's decision. Check every experience claim against evidence. Keep prose short enough to skim once, and stop after the last useful fact.
